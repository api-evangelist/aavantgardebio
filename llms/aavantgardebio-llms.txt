## What this company is

- Clinical-stage gene therapy company, founded 2021 by Prof. Alberto Auricchio, headquartered in Milan, Italy.
- Two proprietary AAV vector platforms — dual hybrid and dual intein — that split a large
  expression cassette across two AAV8 vectors so it can exceed single-vector cargo capacity.
- AAVB-081: dual hybrid AAV therapy for retinitis pigmentosa due to Usher syndrome type 1B
  (MYO7A). LUCE-1 Phase 1/2; the first dual AAV gene therapy tested clinically in an ocular indication.
- AAVB-039: dual intein AAV therapy for ABCA4-associated Stargardt disease. CELESTE Phase 1/2;
  FDA Fast Track and Orphan Drug designation.
- STELLA: natural history study.
- Funding: EUR 61 million Series A, USD 141 million Series B.
